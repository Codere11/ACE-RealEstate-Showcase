1. INSTA-MEETING EXPERIENCE
===============================================================================

Why ACE should be BETTER than Zoom/Calendly for B2B sales meetings:

  Zoom/Calendly problem: Visitor fills "Contact Us" form → gets email 3 days later
  → "Let's hop on a Zoom next Thursday" → Thursday comes → "Can you see my screen?"
  → 15 minutes wasted on intros → meeting ends, no follow-up → lead goes cold.

  ACE experience: Visitor lands on site → ACE greets them → 2 turns of qualification
  → "Our team is available RIGHT NOW. Want to talk?" → Salesperson clicks JOIN →
  video appears IN THE SAME WIDGET, no install, no link, no "can you hear me" →
  salesperson already knows their name, company, budget, problem → 15 productive
  minutes → meeting ends → notes, next steps, profile saved in dashboard.

THE EDGE: Zero friction for the buyer. The meeting starts where the conversation
already is. No context switch. No "let me send you a link." No "download Zoom."
The chat widget IS the meeting room.

--- What makes this better than Calendly ---

  Calendly: "Pick a time next Tuesday." Prospect leaves, forgets, no-shows.
  ACE: "They're here NOW. Talk to them NOW." Captures impulse interest.

  Calendly: Salesperson gets a calendar invite. No context. "Who is this?"
  ACE: Salesperson sees full chat history, extracted profile (company, budget,
  problem), qualification score. Joins the conversation already informed.

  Calendly: Meeting ends. Nothing happens. Manual CRM entry.
  ACE: Meeting auto-logged. Profile fields auto-extracted. Funnel metrics updated.
  Follow-up scheduling suggested.

--- What makes this better than Zoom ---

  Zoom: Separate app. Link. Waiting room. "You're muted." "Can you share?"
  ACE: In-browser video via LiveKit. No install. No link. No account. The video
  just appears in the widget they're already looking at.

  Zoom: No qualification. Salesperson starts every call cold.
  ACE: AI pre-qualifies. Salesperson joins mid-conversation, already briefed.

  Zoom: Generic. Works for everything from family calls to board meetings.
  ACE: Purpose-built for B2B sales qualification calls. The entire UX is designed
  around THIS use case: qualify → connect → close.

--- What needs to be enhanced to make it FEEL premium ---

  A. VISITOR SIDE (the "wow" moment):

     [NOW]  Staff clicks JOIN → text takeover. Video is separate (manual go-live).
     [WANT] Single "JOIN CALL" button for staff that does text + video together.
            One click. Text takeover + LiveKit room created + video starts.

     [NOW]  Video appears abruptly or requires manual endpoint check.
     [WANT] "Ringing" animation — AI says "Povezujem vas z ekipo..." → subtle
            pulse animation → video fades in over 1.5 seconds → "● V ŽIVO" badge.
            Feels like FaceTime connecting, not like a WebRTC debug screen.

     [NOW]  No audio indicator. Visitor doesn't know if the salesperson can hear.
     [WANT] Subtle audio waveform when salesperson is speaking. Mute indicator
            when they're not. "Ekipa se je pridružila" toast.

     [NOW]  Post-meeting: nothing. Chat goes silent.
     [WANT] After meeting ends: "Hvala za pogovor! Povzetek srečanja in naslednji
            koraki bodo poslani na vaš email." Auto-generated meeting summary
            (LLM reads transcript, writes 3 bullet points + next steps).

  B. SALESPERSON SIDE (the "holy shit this is easy" moment):

     [NOW]  Dashboard shows leads. Salesperson manually monitors.
     [WANT] Browser notification + sound when a qualified lead is chatting.
            "🟢 Qualified lead: Marko from TechCorp — €50K budget — wants AI
            reception. Click to join." One click → they're in the conversation.

     [NOW]  No mobile. Dashboard is desktop-only.
     [WANT] Mobile-responsive dashboard. Salesperson can join a takeover from
            their phone. "Ping — hot lead on site. Tap to join."

     [NOW]  Post-meeting: manual notes.
     [WANT] After meeting: auto-generated summary appears in the lead thread.
            "Call summary: Marko needs AI reception for 3 clinics. Budget €40-60K.
            Timeline: Q3. Next step: demo next Tuesday. ACE generated this."

     [NOW]  No CRM integration.
     [WANT] Webhook/push to HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive. Meeting logged
            automatically. Contact created if new. Deal stage updated.

  C. BOOKING EXPERIENCE (for when team is offline):

     [NOW]  ace_schedule_call books a 30-min slot. Basic.
     [WANT] Calendar picker shows REAL team availability (Google Calendar /
            Outlook sync). Visitor picks a slot → confirmed instantly → calendar
            invites sent to both parties. Like Calendly, but inside the chat widget.

     [NOW]  Meeting type is always "Discovery Call."
     [WANT] Configurable meeting types per org: "30-min demo", "15-min intro",
            "60-min technical deep-dive." Sales team sets their available hours
            per meeting type.

  D. ANALYTICS (closing the loop):

     [NOW]  Dashboard shows leads and bookings. Analize tab spec exists.
     [WANT] Analize tab BUILT and working. Sales manager opens it, types
            "Kako gre ta teden?" → AI responds: "12 qualified leads, 7 meetings
            booked, 4 completed, 2 no-shows. Conversion rate 58%. Top drop-off:
            price objection at €299/mo. Top performer: Maja (5 meetings, 4 closed)."

     [WANT] Meeting quality metrics: average call duration, talk/listen ratio,
            follow-up rate, time-to-close. ACE knows these because it's IN the
            meeting pipeline, unlike Calendly which stops at "meeting booked."

2. PRICING & CONCRETE OFFER
===============================================================================

The product replaces or augments an SDR. An SDR costs $50-80K/year in the US.
ACE should price at a fraction of that — but enough to be a serious SaaS business.

--- Pricing Model: Tiered Monthly SaaS ---

  STARTER      $149/mo    (or $1,490/yr — 2 months free)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1 salesperson seat
  Up to 100 AI-qualified leads/mo
  Standard qualifier (customizable)
  Text takeover
  Basic analytics
  Email support
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  For: Solo founders, small agencies, first sales hire.

  PRO          $499/mo    (or $4,990/yr)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  5 salesperson seats
  Up to 500 AI-qualified leads/mo
  Custom qualifier + A/B testing
  Text + VIDEO takeover (LiveKit)
  Discovery call scheduling
  Full analytics + Analize tab
  Slack/email notifications
  Priority support
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  For: B2B startups with a small sales team. The sweet spot.

  BUSINESS     $1,499/mo  (or $14,990/yr)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  20 salesperson seats
  Unlimited leads
  Everything in PRO, plus:
  CRM integration (HubSpot, Salesforce)
  Calendar sync (Google, Outlook)
  Auto-generated meeting summaries
  Multi-org (agencies, holding companies)
  API access
  Dedicated support
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  For: Established B2B companies, agencies managing multiple clients.

  ENTERPRISE   Custom
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Unlimited everything
  White-label
  On-premise deployment option
  Custom integrations
  SLA with uptime guarantee
  Dedicated account manager
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  For: Large enterprises, high-volume sales orgs.

--- Why Monthly SaaS (not per-lead, not per-meeting) ---

  1. Predictable revenue. B2B companies budget monthly, not per-lead.
  2. No "did this meeting come from ACE?" attribution fights.
  3. SDRs are a fixed cost. ACE replaces part of that fixed cost with a smaller
     fixed cost. The math is easy: $149/mo vs $4,000/mo for an SDR.
  4. Usage-based pricing creates a disincentive: "should I let ACE talk to this
     lead or save it for a better one?" Monthly flat fee removes that hesitation.
  5. Simpler sales process: "It's $499/mo. Try it free for 14 days."

--- The Concrete Offer ---

  "ACE replaces your 'Contact Us' form with an AI SDR that qualifies leads and
  books insta-meetings with your sales team. Try it free for 14 days. No credit
  card. Your AI goes live on your site in 5 minutes."

  One-liner for cold outreach:
  "Your website visitors are leaving without talking to sales. ACE puts an AI
  SDR on your site that qualifies leads and connects them to your team INSTANTLY
  — for $149/mo. Better than a contact form. Cheaper than an SDR."

  Value prop in numbers:
  "Average B2B website converts 2% of visitors to leads. Of those, maybe 20%
  get a meeting. That's 0.4% of traffic → meetings. ACE captures conversations
  from visitors who would have bounced. If it books ONE extra meeting per month
  that closes, it pays for itself 10x over."

--- Pricing psychology notes ---

  - Don't go below $99. Below $99 it feels like a toy, not an enterprise tool.
  - The jump from $149 to $499 is intentional. It's 3.3x but unlocks video.
    Video takeover is the killer feature. Most teams will want it.
  - Annual billing should be the default. "Save 17% with annual."
  - No "Contact Sales" for Pro. Friction kills SaaS conversion. Make the pricing
    page self-serve for everything except Enterprise.
  - 14-day trial, not 7. B2B sales cycles are slow. They need time to see results.
  - No feature gating on leads during trial. Let them see the full value.

3. END-TO-END TESTING BEFORE GO-LIVE
===============================================================================

--- Test Matrix ---

  A. FULL FLOW (happy path):

     1. Visitor opens /demo → ACE greets in chat
     2. Visitor types "Rabimo AI za prodajo, proračun ~30K"
     3. ACE extracts: budget=30K, problem=AI za prodajo → qualifies as hot lead
     4. ACE: "Bi želeli da se naša ekipa takoj vključi v pogovor?"
     5. Visitor: "Ja, prosim."
     6. ACE calls ace_request_team → event published → lead turns green in dashboard
     7. Salesperson (logged into dashboard) sees notification → clicks JOIN
     8. Salesperson's message appears in chat as "STAFF" role
     9. Salesperson clicks "Go Live" → LiveKit room created → video appears on visitor side
     10. 5-minute conversation happens (text + video)
     11. Salesperson clicks "End Live" → video fades out
     12. Salesperson clicks "End Takeover" → AI resumes
     13. Lead profile updated: business_name, budget, problem, meeting logged
     14. Booking appears in REZERVACIJE tab
     ☐ TESTED

  B. OFFLINE FLOW (team closed):

     1. Visitor opens chat outside 9-17 Mon-Fri
     2. ACE: "Trenutno smo zaprti. Delovni čas: pon-pet 9-17."
     3. Visitor: "Rabim klic jutri."
     4. ACE books discovery call for tomorrow 10:00 via ace_schedule_call
     5. Booking appears in dashboard calendar
     ☐ TESTED

  C. VISITOR WHO DOESN'T WANT TAKEOVER:

     1. ACE offers live team → visitor says "Ne, samo informacije rabim"
     2. ACE continues answering questions, doesn't push takeover again immediately
     3. After 2 more turns, ACE gently offers ace_schedule_call
     ☐ TESTED

  D. WHAT IF SALESPERSON DOESN'T JOIN:

     1. ACE calls ace_request_team → event published
     2. 60 seconds pass, no staff message appears
     3. ACE should time out and say: "Ekipa je trenutno zasedena. Lahko vam
        rezerviram termin za klic jutri ob 10h?"
     4. System needs a timeout — currently ace_request_team just publishes an event
        and assumes someone will join. If no one does, visitor is stuck.
     ☐ NOT IMPLEMENTED — ADD TIMEOUT

  E. WHAT IF VISITOR LEAVES MID-CONVERSATION:

     1. Visitor closes tab during qualification
     2. Lead stays in DB with status OPEN_CHAT
     3. Salesperson sees "no response" indicator in dashboard
     4. System should offer "send follow-up email" if contact exists
     ☐ PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED — ADD RE-ENGAGEMENT

  F. LIVELKIT FAILURE:

     1. Go-live clicked → LiveKit server is down
     2. What happens? Error toast? Fallback to text-only takeover?
     ☐ TEST AND HANDLE GRACEFULLY

  G. MULTI-TENANT ISOLATION:

     1. Org A (slug: "techcorp") and Org B (slug: "agency") both have live qualifiers
     2. Visitor on /techcorp sees TechCorp's AI
     3. Visitor on /agency sees Agency's AI
     4. Leads don't leak between orgs
     5. Staff from Org A can't see Org B's leads
     ☐ TESTED

  H. CONCURRENT CONVERSATIONS:

     1. 10 visitors chatting simultaneously on the same org
     2. AI responses don't slow down significantly
     3. Event polling doesn't mix up SIDs
     4. Dashboard shows all 10 leads sorted correctly
     ☐ TEST WITH SIMULATOR (scripts/simulate_b2b.py)

  I. BROWSER COMPATIBILITY:

     1. Chrome (latest) — primary
     2. Firefox (latest)
     3. Safari (latest) — especially LiveKit video
     4. Mobile Chrome (Android)
     5. Mobile Safari (iOS) — especially chat input + video
     ☐ TEST MANUALLY

  J. SEO / PAGE LOAD:

     1. The Angular SPA should NOT hurt page load metrics
     2. Chat widget should lazy-load (don't block initial render)
     3. The SPA fallback in main.py should return proper status codes
     4. Add <meta> tags for social preview
     ☐ REVIEW

  K. GDPR / PRIVACY:

     2. Data retention policy: how long are conversations stored?
     3. Right to deletion: can a visitor request their data be deleted?
     4. Data processing agreement (DPA) available for EU customers
     ☐ ADDRESS BEFORE EU LAUNCH

4. MARKET: NOT SLOVENIA
===============================================================================

--- Why Slovenia is the wrong primary market ---

  1. MARKET SIZE: Slovenia has ~2M people. The number of B2B companies with
     meaningful website traffic AND a dedicated salesperson is maybe 500-1,000.
     Total addressable market: ~$500K/year if you capture 100% of them. Realistic:
     maybe $50K/year. Not enough to build a company on.

  2. BUSINESS CULTURE: As the CEO you met today confirmed — "In Slovenia, two
     phone calls can get you to almost anyone." B2B in Slovenia is relationship-
     driven. Deals happen through personal networks, not inbound website traffic.
     The problem ACE solves (visitors who never talk to sales) is simply smaller
     in a market where everyone knows everyone.

  3. TOO SMALL FOR VC: A Slovenian-only SaaS can be a nice lifestyle business
     (€50-100K ARR), but it won't attract investment. The market cap is the
     population cap.

  4. LANGUAGE TRAP: Building in Slovenian is a dead end for growth. You can't
     expand to Croatia (different language), Serbia (different), Austria (German).
     English-first from day one unlocks the entire Western market.

--- What Slovenia IS good for ---

  1. INITIAL TESTING: The product was built with Slovenian. The demo works.
     You have access to Slovenian business owners for feedback. Use them as
     design partners, not as the target market.

  2. REFERENCE CUSTOMERS: Get 3-5 Slovenian companies using ACE for free/cheap.
     They give testimonials, case studies, and bug reports. Their logos go on
     the landing page. "Trusted by B2B companies across Europe" sounds better
     than "Trusted by no one."

  3. CHEAP ITERATION: Fixing bugs and improving UX is faster and cheaper when
     your design partners are in the same time zone and speak your language.

--- The real market: USA + English-speaking B2B ---

  WHY USA:

  1. MARKET SIZE: ~300,000 B2B SaaS companies alone. Add agencies, consulting
     firms, professional services — addressable market is 500K+ companies.
     Even 0.1% penetration = 500 customers = $75K-$750K MRR depending on mix.

  2. SDR IS A KNOWN COST: US companies know what an SDR costs ($50-80K/year
     fully loaded). "Replace your SDR with AI for $499/mo" is an instant ROI
     conversation. In Slovenia, many companies don't even have a dedicated SDR.

  3. INBOUND MARKETING CULTURE: US B2B companies spend heavily on content,
     SEO, and ads to drive website traffic. They FEEL the pain of visitors
     who don't convert. They already use Calendly, Drift, HubSpot chat. ACE
     is a better version of tools they already pay for.

  4. ENGLISH-FIRST: No localization needed for the initial market. The product
     already uses OpenAI which is strongest in English. Qualifiers, prompts,
     documentation — all English.

  5. SAAS BUYING MATURITY: US companies are used to: find tool online → free
     trial → self-serve onboarding → swipe credit card. Low-touch sales motion
     works. In Slovenia, you need a phone call and an invoice.

  SECONDARY MARKETS (after US is established):

  - UK, Canada, Australia: Same language, similar B2B culture, smaller but
    still meaningful markets. Good for expansion without localization cost.
  - DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland): Strong B2B, high willingness to pay.
    Requires German localization of the qualifier and chat UI. Worth it at scale.
  - Nordics: Tech-savvy, English-friendly, high GDP per capita.

--- Market Entry Strategy ---

  PHASE 0 (NOW): Slovenia — design partners & reference customers
    - 3-5 Slovenian B2B companies using ACE for free
    - Gather feedback, fix bugs, polish UX
    - Build case studies in English (translate Slovenian results)
    - Duration: 4-6 weeks

  PHASE 1: US launch — English-only, self-serve
    - Landing page at ace.ai (or similar domain)
    - 14-day free trial, no credit card
    - Target: B2B SaaS companies with 1-20 salespeople
    - Channels: Product Hunt, LinkedIn ads, cold email, B2B SaaS directories
    - Goal: 50 paying customers, $25K MRR
    - Duration: 3-6 months

  PHASE 2: Scale US + add UK/Canada/Australia
    - Content marketing: "AI SDR benchmarks," "Calendly vs ACE," case studies
    - Outbound sales for Business/Enterprise tiers
    - CRM integrations (HubSpot first — most popular with SMBs)
    - Goal: 500 customers, $250K MRR
    - Duration: 6-12 months

  PHASE 3: DACH expansion (if US is working)
    - German localization of qualifier + chat UI
    - Berlin/Munich B2B focus
    - Requires German-speaking sales or local partner

--- The Slovenia Exit Strategy ---

  Don't abandon Slovenian support. Keep it as a "look how flexible our AI is"
  demo. But do these things now:

  1. Make English the DEFAULT qualifier language for new orgs. Keep Slovenian
     as an option. The seed script creates the demo org in Slovenian for testing,
     but new orgs created via admin panel get an English qualifier.

  2. Translate the dashboard UI to English (it's already mostly English from
     Angular, but check labels, buttons, error messages).

  3. Buy an English domain (ace.ai, tryace.ai, useace.ai, etc.).

  4. Write ALL future docs, landing pages, and sales materials in English first.
     Slovenian is for dev/testing only from this point forward.
