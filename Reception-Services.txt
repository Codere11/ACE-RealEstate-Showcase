# ACE — Product Goal

## North Star

Make ACE a **no-brainer** for B2B companies that want to capture and qualify leads from their website — automatically, instantly, with AI.

## What ACE Is

An AI SDR that lives on your B2B website. It greets visitors, qualifies them in 2-3 turns, and pushes for an instant live meeting with your sales team. If the team is offline, it books a discovery call for the next working day.

## Core Value

- **No forms** — visitors chat, AI qualifies
- **Insta-meetings** — qualified leads get a live salesperson within 2 turns
- **24/7 coverage** — AI works when your team sleeps
- **Multi-tenant** — each B2B client is a separate org with its own qualifier

## Target Customers

B2B companies with at least one salesperson. Anyone who:
- Gets website traffic from potential buyers
- Has a sales team that wants more qualified conversations
- Is tired of "Contact Us" forms that go nowhere
