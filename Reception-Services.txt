# ACE Reception Services — Project Goal

## North Star

Make ACE a **no-brainer** for Slovenian beauty salons (kozmeticni saloni) and similar appointment-based service businesses.

## Why Beauty Salons

They already have the perfect business model to digitalize: someone walks in → talks with the receptionist → asks a few questions → selects a service → gets it done. ACE replicates this exact flow online with an AI receptionist.

Higher transaction volume = faster revenue via commissions.

## What We're Building

### Visitor-Side Demo (3 services, basic AI-generated photos)

An AI receptionist sits middle-bottom of the page — highly inviting opening message. It can:
- Greet and guide visitors conversationally
- Navigate them to relevant sections of the page, explaining services
- Answer questions about services, pricing, availability
- Help select and book a service

**Open/Closed hours awareness:**
- **"We are open — a human can join you"** — staff can hop into the conversation
- **"We are closed — but I can handle the basics"** — AI covers everything until staff returns

### Customer Controls for Human Handoff

- **Accept/Deny** when a staff member wants to join (some visitors don't want it)
- **Request a staff member** — the visitor can explicitly ask for a human
- When staff joins: a slow, premium fade-in of the black camera rectangle — feels like "streaming tokens," not a jarring pop-in

### Agent/Salon Dashboard

- Calendar + appointment management
- Qualified lead overview
- Handoff controls (join/leave conversations)
- Service and hours management

## The Feel

Like walking into a real store with a real receptionist — just digital. Instantly relatable. Instantly understandable. A no-brainer for any salon owner.
