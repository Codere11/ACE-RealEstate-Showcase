Document for Pi Agent: VAIKARDD AI Spatial e-Counter Project
Date: 18 May 2026
Project Name: ProstorAI e-Counter (VAIKARDD AI Spatial e-Counter)
Base: ACE-RealEstate-Showcase (by Codere11) + Omsoft GIS expertise
Goal: Create a strong, fundable prototype and proposal for the VAIKARDD national geospatial digital twin project.

1. High-Level Vision
We are transforming the existing ACE-RealEstate-Showcase into a national conversational AI access layer for Slovenia’s geospatial digital twin.
Core Experience
A citizen, business, or public official opens a clean, branded web portal (national or municipal). They type or speak naturally in Slovenian:
“Pokaži parcelo 123/456 v Ljubljani” or “Kakšna so tveganja za gradnjo na tej lokaciji?”
The system instantly:

Pulls live authoritative data from GURS digital twin (cadastre, mass valuation, market records, LiDAR risks, 3D models, zoning…),
Shows an interactive map with overlays,
Answers conversationally with clear explanations,
Qualifies the user’s intent,
Offers one-click live handoff to a human officer or starts an e-application.

Key Characteristics

Multi-tenant: National (GURS) + per-municipality scoping.
Sovereign AI (self-hosted models, no foreign commercial APIs).
Built on proven ACE architecture: Java Spring Boot frontend + Python/LangGraph intelligent orchestration + LiveKit handoff.
Focus on real usability for citizens, businesses, planners, and crisis teams.

This turns the backend digital twin infrastructure into a practical, intelligent public service — exactly what VAIKARDD is missing.

Budget: €9.39 million (€5.35M ERDF)
Status (18 May 2026): Early implementation / ramp-up phase (GreenSLO4D ends 30 June 2026).
Lead: GURS (Geodetic Administration of Slovenia).

Build a true national geospatial digital twin (3D/4D, real-time monitoring, simulations, AI/ML).
Deliver advanced digital services for different user groups (citizens, businesses, public administration).
Improve spatial planning, real estate management, risk/crisis management, and sustainable development.
Create user-friendly access and interaction with the digital twin (not just raw data portals).

Why This Is a Perfect Fit

ACE already has the complete visitor → AI qualification → handoff → workflow engine.
The biggest current gap in VAIKARDD is the intelligent, conversational user layer.
We combine Omsoft’s 20+ years of GIS/public sector delivery with a ready, production-oriented codebase.
Timing is excellent: They are building the project team now and need concrete solutions for user services in H2 2026.
Strong alignment with Slovenian/EU priorities (digital sovereignty, INSPIRE, Digital Slovenia 2030).

This gives us a realistic path to government/EU funding without starting from zero.

What Exactly VAIKARDD Wants — Realistic Picture Based on Official Goals and Similar Projects
Official Core Objectives (from GURS announcements, Oct 2025)
VAIKARDD aims to build a national geospatial digital twin of Slovenia that goes far beyond static data viewers. Key elements include:

Real-time monitoring, analysis, simulation, and prediction of spatial changes.
Advanced digital services and solutions using geospatial data, AI/ML, multidimensional (3D/4D) models, modern visualizations (including VR/AR potential).
User-friendly access for diverse groups: citizens, businesses, planners, public officials, researchers, and crisis teams.
Concrete applications in spatial planning, real estate management, disaster/crisis response, sustainable development, and policy making.
Full integration with existing infrastructure (eProstor, SLO4D/GreenSLO4D) + improved interoperability (national + EU/INSPIRE).
Emphasis on digital sovereignty, data security, and innovation in geoinformatics.

In short: They are building the backend infrastructure (high-quality data, LiDAR, 3D models, archives) and now urgently need practical, intelligent front-end services that make the digital twin actually usable and valuable in everyday workflows.
What This Most Likely Looks Like in Practice (Lessons from Similar EU Digital Twin Projects)
Successful geospatial/city digital twin projects (Rotterdam, Munich, Rennes, Destination Earth components, Leipzig, Burgas, etc.) show a clear pattern of what works and gets funded/expanded:

Conversational & Intelligent Access Layer (the biggest gap everywhere)
Citizens and businesses don’t want another map portal. They want natural-language interaction: “Show me risks on this parcel” or “Simulate impact of new building here.” AI qualifies intent and delivers personalized, understandable answers + visualizations.
High-Impact, Focused Use-Cases First
Top priorities in similar projects:
Property/parcel inquiries with enriched data (cadastre + valuation + risks + 3D).
Risk & crisis management (flood, erosion, fire overlays with real-time alerts).
Planning simulations (“what-if” scenarios for zoning or development).
Investor/business location analysis.
Streamlined e-services (pre-filled applications with auto data pull).

Multi-Level & Multi-Role Access
Citizen-friendly simple interface.
Professional/officer dashboards with deeper analytics and handoff.
Municipal vs. national scoping (exactly like our multi-tenant approach).

Seamless Human-AI Collaboration
AI handles 80% of routine queries; easy escalation to live officer with full context (conversation history + spatial data). This dramatically improves efficiency and user satisfaction.
Visualization & Simulation
Interactive maps + 3D/LiDAR previews + basic what-if tools are extremely effective for engagement and decision-making.
Measurable Outcomes That Matter to Funders
Reduced processing time for public inquiries.
Higher citizen engagement and self-service rates.
Faster crisis response.
Better data-driven planning.
Demonstrable ROI on EU money (user numbers, satisfaction, efficiency gains).
